## Final prompt set

### Retail background

Generate one polished editorial photograph for a SAP Retail Signal business pitch presentation. Landscape 16:9. A contemporary premium apparel store with neatly arranged cream, blue and navy garments, customers softly blurred in the background, natural window light, restrained cool-blue and ivory palette, realistic retail architecture. Focus on the clothing rails and a store associate using a tablet on the right half. No readable screens, no logos, no text, no watermarks. Professional enterprise brand photography, clean composition, credible ordinary store rather than futuristic holograms. Leave the left third visually quiet for potential overlay. Output a single photograph.

### Bob portrait

Generate one editorial photographic portrait for a fictional customer persona in an enterprise retail pitch deck. Portrait or square composition. Bob is a fictional male regional merchandise planner in his early forties, dark short hair, subtle stubble, light blue shirt with sleeves rolled, standing at a desk in a modern apparel retail back office, garment samples behind him, looking thoughtfully at an open laptop and printed inventory reports. Approachable and realistic, not exaggerated stress, natural cool daylight, blue and ivory palette. No readable text, no logos, no watermarks. Subject face and upper body clearly visible, leave some breathing room around him. This is an illustrative fictional persona, not a real client.

### Garment RFID

Generate one high-end editorial close-up photograph for a corporate presentation about garment RFID hardware. Landscape 3:2 composition. A cream cotton shirt on a clean retail fitting-room rack, a small ordinary white radio-frequency identification garment hangtag in clear focus, discreet fixed reader hardware above the fitting-room entrance in the soft background. Neutral ivory surfaces and subtle blue accent lighting. Credible existing retail technology, no futuristic effects, no camera surveillance. No printed words, no logos, no readable screens or tag numbers, no watermarks. Photorealistic, premium product photography.

### Fulfillment

One editorial enterprise photography image, landscape 16:9. A modern apparel distribution centre, shelves of neutral clothing cartons, a warehouse associate scanning a parcel with an ordinary handheld barcode scanner, clearly visible packing and fulfillment process. Cool clean blue and ivory palette with warm natural accents, bright realistic light, organized and credible, no futuristic effects. Composition places the associate on the right third, with shelves receding left. No text, no readable labels, no logos, no watermarks. Use as an illustrative supply-chain background in a retailer pitch.
